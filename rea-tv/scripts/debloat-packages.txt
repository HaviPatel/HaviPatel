# REA TV debloat list for Amazon Fire TV.
#
# Built from the list tested on the Fire TV Stick 4K 1st gen (AFTMM, Fire OS 6.7.1.1, NS6711/5908) in
# cleanyourtv, examples/amazon-fire-tv-stick-4k-2018 (MIT, https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv):
# applied on two units of that model and build, survived reboots, nothing had to be re-enabled.
# On other Fire OS versions, packages that don't exist are skipped and protected ones are reported.
#
# Format: tier|package|what it is
#   1 = telemetry, ads, sponsored rows, promos (safe for everyone)
#   2 = Amazon apps and services you may not use (Live tab, Kids, Music, Photos, Silk, Alexa extras)
#   3 = things you'd notice: Alexa voice search, Prime Video, casting, the Appstore
#
# Only ever disabled with `pm disable-user --user 0` (fully reversible: scripts/rea.sh restore, or a
# factory reset). Amazon's launcher, setup, settings, keyboard and core services are not listed, and
# the scripts refuse to touch them.
#
# Protected on Fire OS 6 (pm disable-user is refused without root), so not listed:
# com.amazon.client.metrics, com.amazon.device.metrics, com.amazon.minerva.client.api,
# com.amazon.device.logmanager, com.amazon.device.crashmanager, com.amazon.diode,
# com.fireos.usagestats.proxy, com.amazon.ftvads.deeplinking, com.amazon.tv.csapp, com.amazon.tv.oobe,
# com.amazon.tcomm, com.amazon.securitysyncclient, com.amazon.whisperlink.core.android, and the OTA updater.
#
# Debloating cannot remove the sponsored banner on Amazon's own home (its launcher fetches it from
# amazon-adsystem.com). With REA TV as your home you don't see it; otherwise block *.amazon-adsystem.com
# on your router, or set an ad-blocking DNS through a static IP in the Fire TV's Wi-Fi settings
# (Private DNS needs Android 9; Fire OS 6 is Android 7.1).
